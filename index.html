What is it?

A Domain Authority Checker gives you 3 numbers

DA — Domain Authority, whole website, 1–100

PA — Page Authority, one single page, 1–100

Spam Score — how spammy the links look, 1%–100%

With these three numbers you can judge a site's link strength — and decide if a backlink from it is worth it. This free DA checker shows all three in seconds.

1 — Domain Authority

DA: a score from 1 to 100, created by Moz

DA predicts how likely a whole website is to rank — based on its backlink profile.

The scale is logarithmic: moving from DA 70 to 80 is far harder than from 10 to 20.

New websites always start at DA 1.

Important: Google does NOT use DA. It is Moz's prediction — useful for comparing websites, not a ranking factor.

2 — Page Authority

PA: the same idea, for ONE page

DA measures the whole domain. PA measures one single URL.

A site can have high DA and still have pages with low PA — because PA depends on links to that exact page.

Always check both: DA tells you about the site, PA tells you about the page.

3 — Spam Score

Spam Score: a warning sign, in %

Moz compares a site's links with the links of penalised sites.

1% – 30% — Low, safe

31% – 60% — Medium, be careful

61% – 100% — High, warning sign

Before you take a backlink — or buy a domain — check its Spam Score first. A free spam score checker shows this number with DA and PA.

Don't mix them up

DA vs DR vs Authority Score

DA — Moz, 1–100

DR — Ahrefs Domain Rating, 0–100

AS — Semrush Authority Score, 1–100

Same site, three different numbers — and nothing is wrong. They use different data.

Rule: always compare websites using the SAME tool.

What is a good score?

There is no single good DA

DA is relative — compare with the sites ranking for YOUR keywords.

20 – 30 — strong for a small niche blog

40 – 50 — strong for most small & medium sites

60+ — large, established websites

If the sites ranking for your keyword are DA 25–35, a DA of 40 is more than enough.

How to improve DA

Earn better backlinks

Create linkable content. Earn links from trusted, relevant sites. Remove toxic links. Be patient — real movement takes 3–6 months.

Never buy links — it violates Google's spam policies.
